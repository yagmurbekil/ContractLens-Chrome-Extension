Acme Cloud Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us when creating an account, updating your profile, or contacting customer support. This includes your name, email address, billing address, and usage activity across our services.

2. Data Sharing and Third Parties

We may share your personal information with third-party advertising partners, tracking networks, and affiliated entities to deliver customized advertisements and cross-platform tracking profiles.

3. Data Retention

We store your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide you the services. When you delete your account, we may retain certain diagnostic logs in backup archives for compliance purposes.

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact our legal compliance team at privacy@acmecloud.example.com.
